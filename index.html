TANKERS — what the number means

N tankers = N additional Starship launches that transfer propellant to the crewed ship in LEO (not larger tanks). Total launches = 1 + N. Ship-to-ship cryogenic transfer has not yet been demonstrated; per-tanker load (100–150 t) and boil-off are public estimates.
