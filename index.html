What does “tankers” mean?

Each tanker is another Starship launch that docks with the crewed ship in low Earth orbit and tops up its tanks. Total launches = 1 + tankers. Ship-to-ship propellant transfer has not been demonstrated yet, and boil-off is not modelled.
