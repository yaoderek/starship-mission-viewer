What are “tankers”?

Starship can't reach other planets on the propellant left after launching to orbit. The plan is to refuel it in low Earth orbit: N tankers means N extra Starship launches that each carry propellant up and pump it into the ship (or a depot) — not bigger tanks. Total launches = 1 ship + N tankers.

Ship-to-ship cryogenic propellant transfer in orbit has not yet been demonstrated (so far only an internal tank-to-tank transfer on flight test 3). Per-tanker delivery (100–150 t) and boil-off are public estimates.
